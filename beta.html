Beta testers wanted

Help us bring Clara to Google Play

Clara is a free, private AI assistant that runs on your own PC, with an Android app to talk to her. To publish the app on Google Play, we need a small group of testers to use the test version for two weeks. Want in?

Join the beta

Free. Takes two minutes. Leave any time.

1What you'll need

  • An Android phone (Android 9 or newer) with a Google account
  • Ideally a Linux PC with an NVIDIA graphics card (12 GB of memory or more) to run Clara on. No such PC? You can still help: install the test app and tell us what you think of it.

2How it works

  1. Join the beta with the button above. Use the Google account that's on your phone.
  2. We send you the Google Play test link. Open it on your phone and tap Become a tester.
  3. Install Clara from Google Play and, if you have the PC, set it up with the install guide.
  4. Stay in the test for at least 14 days (just keep the app installed) and tell us what works and what doesn't.

3Feedback

Email clara@thewiderlens.info, use Report this reply (long-press a message in the app), or open an issue on GitHub.

Your privacy: we only use your email to add you to the test and send you test updates. Clara itself sends nothing to us: see the privacy policy.
